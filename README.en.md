# MeoWGuard · Install through the browser

🌐 **[Ler em português](README.md)**

Page to install the games you bought on the Nintendo Switch, through the browser and the USB cable,
using the **MeoWGuard** app.

**Address:** https://techmeow.app/MeoWGuard/en/ · also in [Español](https://techmeow.app/MeoWGuard/es/) and [Русский](https://techmeow.app/MeoWGuard/ru/)

## How to use

1. On the Switch: open MeoWGuard → **Install games** → **Mac** → **Through the Chrome browser**.
2. On the computer (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Opera): open the page, click
   **Connect to the Switch** and choose **Nintendo Switch** in the list.
3. Choose the files (NSP, NSZ, XCI, XCZ or MSP) and click **Send to the Switch**.
   The Switch installs them one by one.

Safari and Firefox do not have WebUSB. On a Mac, you can also use the free app
[OpenMTP](https://openmtp.ganeshrvel.com).
